# Methods

This document describes the physics and numerics implemented in `src/engine/`. Equations are given in the form used by the code. SI units throughout.

## 1. Gas model (`gas.ts`, `equilibrium.ts`)

**Species data.** NASA CEA 9-coefficient polynomials for Cp°/R, H°/RT and S°/R of Ar, CO₂, H₂O, N₂, O₂, CO, H₂, OH, NO, O and H, taken from pyCycle's `janaf` set (`src/data/janaf.json`). Two temperature ranges per species (200–1000 K, 1000–6000 K).

**Composition.** Element amounts b = {N, O, Ar, C, H} in kmol per kg of mixture, identical to pyCycle's `b0` vector. Dry air is pyCycle's `CEA_AIR_COMPOSITION`; humid air adds H₂O at the water/air ratio from relative humidity (Magnus saturation pressure). Fuels are defined by their elemental formula, lower heating value and absolute enthalpy as supplied: Jet-A as C₁₂H₂₃ (pyCycle's definition), SAF as C₁₂H₂₆, hydrogen H₂, methane CH₄. Mixing with fuel is a mass-weighted sum of element amounts.

**Frozen mode.** Complete lean combustion: CO₂ = C, H₂O = H/2, O₂ = (O − 2C − H/2)/2, N₂ = N/2, Ar. Mixture h(T) and s(T,P) are mole-weighted sums with the ideal-gas mixing entropy and −R ln(P/P₀).

**Equilibrium mode** (default, used when T ≥ 1000 K). Gibbs free energy is minimised with the element-potential formulation of Gordon & McBride (NASA RP-1311): species moles nᵢ = n·exp(Σⱼ aᵢⱼ πⱼ − cᵢ) with cᵢ = μᵢ°/RT + ln(P/P₀); Newton iteration on the element potentials πⱼ and ln n with element-balance and mole-sum residuals, damped steps, converged to 10⁻¹¹. The frozen composition is the initial guess and inactive elements/species are dropped. Below 1000 K dissociation is below 10⁻⁸ and the frozen result is used. Results are memoised per (T, P).

**Derived properties.** h(T,P), s(T,P), frozen cp and γ, local gas constant R·n, speed of sound (frozen), equilibrium cp = ∂h/∂T|P by central difference. Inversions (T from h, T from s, P from s, isentropic end states) use damped Newton with halving line search.

## 2. Atmosphere and flow states (`atmosphere.ts`, `flow.ts`)

US Standard Atmosphere 1976 to 86 km with geometric-to-geopotential conversion (pyCycle treats altitude as geopotential; the reference cases set that flag). ΔT from ISA shifts temperature at constant pressure.

A flow is (gas, Pt, Tt, ht, W). Static states are obtained at a given Mach number (solve ht − h(Ts,Ps) = ½(M a)² along the isentrope), at a given static pressure (isentropic expansion), or at a given area (subsonic branch, bisection; returns the sonic state when the area is below the choking area). Totals from statics iterate T from h and P from s.

## 3. Components (`components.ts`)

* **Compressor.** Pt₂ = PR·Pt₁; ideal exit on the isentrope; ht₂ = ht₁ + (h_ideal − ht₁)/η. Bleeds are extracted at exit conditions (pyCycle `frac_P = frac_work = 1`); power = W_in·(ht₂ − ht₁). Polytropic efficiency reported from the entropy rise.
* **Turbine.** Main flow and each cooling stream expand separately from their own entry pressure (entry pressure = Pt_out + f·(Pt_in − Pt_out)) to Pt_out with the same isentropic efficiency, then mix by enthalpy (pyCycle's `EnthalpyAndPower`). Design mode finds PR for a required power by bisection on ln PR.
* **Combustor.** Fuel at FAR relative to the air mass; exit enthalpy = (W h_in + W_f h_fuel − (1−η_b) W_f LHV)/(W + W_f); Pt loss ΔP/P. Design mode solves FAR for a target Tt₄ by Newton; off-design burns a given FAR.
* **Nozzle.** Convergent: choked when P_amb is below the sonic static pressure, exit at M = 1 and Ps₉ > P_amb; otherwise expanded to ambient. Convergent–divergent: fully expanded. Gross thrust Fg = W·V₉·C_v + (Ps₉ − P_amb)·A₉ with the ideal exit velocity (pyCycle's convention). The back pressure is capped at 0.995·Pt so areas stay finite in sub-ambient transients.
* **Mixer.** Constant-area mixing of core and bypass streams. One stream's Mach is specified; the other is expanded to the same static pressure (pyCycle's `designed_stream`). Mixed state from mass, impulse (Σ Ps A + W V) and energy in the summed area, subsonic branch by bisection on exit static pressure; then a pressure loss. Off-design holds both areas and reports the static-pressure mismatch as a residual.
* **Propeller.** Actuator disc: P_avail = T·(V₀ + w), w = ½(−V₀ + √(V₀² + 2T/ρA)), solved for T; profile efficiency, gearbox efficiency and gear ratio applied; helical tip Mach reported.
* **Cooling model.** Effectiveness ε(m) = ε_max·m/(m + 0.03) per cooled row with m the row flow fraction; ε_req = (T₄ − T_metal)/(T₄ − T_coolant), T_coolant = T₃ + ΔT_pre-swirl. Inlet (working) flow 1.6 m and exit (non-working) flow 0.6 m. Creep life rule of thumb: 25 000 h at 1200 K, halving every 20 K.

## 4. Design point (`core.ts`, `cycle.ts`)

`evaluate(inputs, provider)` walks the gas path station by station for each architecture. A **provider** supplies component operating points: at the design point compressors use the given PR and η and each turbine's PR is solved for the shaft balance (turbine power × η_mech = compressor power + extraction, or the demanded shaft power for a power turbine). Statics are computed at the sizing Mach numbers, which produces areas that drive the cross-section, 3D geometry and the off-design areas.

Station numbering follows SAE ARP 755A: 0, 2, 21, 13, 22, 25, 3, 4, 45, (47), 5, 6, 7, 9, 17, 19. Performance: net thrust, TSFC, specific thrust, OPR, FAR, efficiencies (thermal from effective jet kinetic power, propulsive 2V₀/(V_eff + V₀) generalised through Fg/W), installed figures, emissions, noise.

## 5. Off-design (`maps.ts`, `offdesign.ts`)

**Maps.** NPSS-style tables (α, Nc, R-line) → (Wc, PR, η) for compressors and (α, Np, PR) → (Wp, η) for turbines, bilinear in the first α slice, scaled through the design point by s_Nc, s_PR = (PR−1)/(PR_map−1), s_η, s_Wc (and s_Np, s_Wp). Corrected flow W√θ/δ and speed N/√θ for compressors; W√Tt/Pt and N/√Tt for turbines (pyCycle's forms). Surge margin = (PR_stall/PR · Wc/Wc_stall − 1)·100 along the speed line; it is negative past the stall line and is never clipped. Below the lowest tabulated speed line the map is extended with Wc ∝ Nc, PR − 1 = Nc²·[(PR_min − 1) − 0.02(1 − Nc)] (the windmilling loss scales with the dynamic head of the through-flow, so a starter-driven compressor keeps a small positive pressure rise at static conditions) and falling efficiency; turbines unchoke below the tabulated PR range with Wp ∝ √(1 − 1/PR²).

**Beyond the tabulated R-lines.** Below the first R-line (stall side) the tables are extrapolated linearly for up to one R-line. Above the last R-line (choke side) the flow saturates, gaining at most half of the last tabulated R-line step, while the pressure ratio and efficiency keep falling and steepen with distance: the throttled branch of a choked compressor, used for up to three R-lines. Any operation beyond the tabulated range is flagged on the component (`offMap`), listed in `offDesign.mapViolations` and reported in the result warnings; it is a model extrapolation, not map data.

**Low-flow losses.** Burner, bypass-duct, mixer and inlet pressure-loss fractions keep their design value (pyCycle's convention) down to 50 % of the design corrected flow at the station that feeds them and scale with the dynamic head (corrected flow squared) below that, so that a sub-idle gas path has a through-flow solution. The validated off-design points all lie above the threshold. The nozzle caps its back pressure at 0.9999·Pt as a numerical guard; a solution resting on that cap has no physical through-flow and is flagged (`subAmbient`) and not reported as converged.

**Unknowns and residuals.** Inlet flow W, bypass ratio, spool speeds N_LP (N_IP) N_HP, FAR, one R-line per compressor, one PR per turbine. Residuals: scaled map flow / actual flow − 1 at every compressor and turbine, shaft power balances, nozzle throat area / design area − 1, mixer static-pressure match, and the throttle target (T4, fuel flow, N_HP, N_LP or thrust). Five unknowns for a turbojet, six for a turboprop (constant-speed power turbine), ten for a two-spool turbofan, twelve for three spools.

**Solver.** Newton with forward-difference Jacobian, step limiting to 50 % of each variable's scale, backtracking line search, rejection of non-finite residuals; a retry from the design guess when a warm start fails; then continuation in throttle, altitude and Mach from the design point in six steps. Outside the extrapolation bands the R-lines (and turbine expansion ratios below 1.001) are clamped and a linear penalty on the clamped distance keeps the system square. The penalty is bookkept apart from the physical flow-matching error of every compressor and turbine: a solution is **converged** only when the residual norm is below 10⁻⁶ **and** every flow-matching error is below 10⁻⁶ **and** no nozzle rests on its back-pressure cap, so the penalty can never cancel a flow mismatch and masquerade as convergence. `offDesign.flowMismatch` reports the largest flow error.

**Unlit burner.** `burnerLit: false` runs the combustor at zero fuel and zero heat release whatever the throttle says (the FAR unknown is parked at a dummy value so the system stays square); the transient start-up uses it before light-off.

**Geometry and bleeds off-design.** Nozzle area multiplier A8, customer bleed, handling bleed (to the bypass duct, or overboard), variable-stator flow gain (map flow × (1 + g(Nc/Nc,des − 1))), and per-component efficiency/flow multipliers used by the thermal model.

**Secondary air system.** At the design point, orifices are sized (Cd·A from the compressible orifice law, choked or unchoked) to pass the design cooling, customer and leakage flows between P3 and their sinks (P4, P45, a fixed customer manifold, ambient). Off-design each flow follows the same law on the current pressures, cooling fractions and customer bleed are recomputed, and the interstage seal (feed between P25 and P3 against the turbine cavity at a fraction of P45) is checked for reversal.

## 6. Transients (`transient.ts`)

Spool dynamics I·ω·dω/dt = P_turbine − P_load with I per spool. At each step the gas path is solved quasi-steadily with speeds held as states (power residuals become the reported imbalance), fuel from the schedule or controller, limited by a ratio-unit Wf/P3 ceiling and floor. Heun (RK2) integration.

**Only converged gas paths drive the dynamics.** Each fixed-speed solve tries the warm start, the last converged state, a sub-idle guess (flow ∝ speed, turbine expansion ratios ∝ speed²) and the design guess. If the corrector still does not converge the integration stops with a warning naming the residual and the components outside their maps; the result carries `complete = false` and `stoppedAt_s`, and the view shows the incomplete trajectory as such (gas-path plots contain converged samples; starter-only mechanical states are identified separately). If the predictor does not converge the step falls back to explicit Euler from the converged corrector; such steps are counted (`eulerSteps`) and marked on the samples.

**Limiter.** The Wf/P3 ceiling and floor are applied to the commanded fuel using P3 of the last converged gas path and iterated against the solved P3 to 0.5 % relative fuel tolerance, with at most ten refinements. Precedence is explicit: with a controller present its acceleration schedule (against HP speed corrected using inlet T₂) and deceleration floor apply when `limiter = 'schedule'` (default), the manual limit and floor of the fuel schedule apply when `limiter = 'manual'`; the other set is ignored and the view disables it. Nothing is limited while the burner is unlit. The default schedule has a start-up branch (1.0× the design ratio unit at 20 % corrected speed, 1.2× at 40 %) before the running-range values.

**Start-up.** A starter torque acts on the HP spool until cut-out, windage/friction ∝ N², a windmilling speed fraction at forward speed. Before light-off the burner is unlit (zero fuel, zero heat release, T₄ = T₃). If no through-flow solution exists at the starter-driven speed (below roughly 15–20 % N₂ for the default turbojet), the step is integrated in the explicit **starter-only regime** (starter torque against friction, no gas-path contribution) and marked `gasPath = false`; such samples carry no gas-path quantities. Light-off requires a converged unlit gas path with N₂ and T₃ above their thresholds; the fuel schedule and the controller demand are then referenced to the light-off instant, starting from the light-off fuel fraction. Hung-start detection reports whether the gas path could be established. *Known limitation:* a high-bypass turbofan start-up does not get past the starter-only regime, because the slowly turning booster has to pass the HPC's flow as a restriction (pressure ratio below 1 far beyond the choke side of its map) and the generic maps do not represent that branch; the view reports it as "no through-flow solution".

**FADEC.** Demand (N₁, N₂ or thrust) ramped to a target; PI on the relative error, WfCmd = Wf_base + Wf_des(Kp·e + Ki∫e), conditional anti-windup at fuel limits (including the minimum fuel); current-state speed feedback, previous-sample thrust feedback, and integration over elapsed time; acceleration schedule of Wf/P3 against corrected HP speed; deceleration floor; handling bleed valve open below a set N₂; variable-stator gain.

**Heat soak and clearance.** Casing and disc temperatures follow T₃ as first-order lags (τ_casing, τ_disc). Clearance δ = δ_cold + α(T_c − T₀)r − [α(T_d − T₀)·0.92 r + δ_cf (N/N_des)²], reported as computed. δ ≤ 0 means blade/casing contact: the sample is flagged (`rub`), the result carries `contact` and `minClearance_mm`, and a warning states that the model is outside its valid range from that point. Relative efficiency delta Δη/η = −k·(max(δ, 0.05 mm) − δ_des)/h on the HPC (the compressor for a turbojet), with efficiency multiplier 1 + Δη/η and flow-capacity multiplier 1 + 0.3Δη/η, referenced to design clearance; the 0.05 mm floor protects the correlation only and is never the reported clearance. The thermal model is illustrative: compressor-temperature-driven clearance only, no turbine coupling and no separate blade-temperature state. Existing component modifiers are retained and multiplied by the thermal corrections. A solved efficiency outside (0, 1] stops the trajectory. The chart reports percent of reference efficiency, not absolute efficiency percentage points.

**Scope.** Completing integration is not an operability verdict. Negative map surge margin produces an explicit warning; surge dynamics are absent. The generic gains, inertias, acceleration schedule and thermal constants are not engine-test calibrations. P3 here is total pressure Pt₃; schedules defined with static Ps₃ cannot be copied directly. Fuel and geometry actuators have no lag, and the controller lacks the complete temperature, speed and pressure protection set of an engine FADEC. Thrust control is a virtual experiment. For a turboprop, only HP spool dynamics are integrated; the power turbine is not a separately integrated free spool. See the independent plausibility audit in [validation.md](validation.md).

## 7. Mission (`mission.ts`)

Segments: take-off and hold (duration), cruise (distance), climb and descent (rate). Aircraft: mass, wing area, CD₀, induced factor k, engine count, installed thrust option. Thrust required per engine = (D + m g·ROC/V)/n with D = q S (CD₀ + k C_L²). Throttle is solved for thrust; if the solution exceeds the N₁, N₂ or T₄ rating the point is re-solved at the limit and the shortfall is reported. Fuel and mass are integrated segment by segment; the aircraft mass defaults to a thrust/weight ratio of 0.28 at sea level for the current engine.

## 8. Design studies

* **Optimiser** (`optimize.ts`): Nelder–Mead over chosen inputs with bound clipping, quadratic penalties for T₃, T₄, minimum thrust, metal temperature and an off-design surge-margin check; objectives TSFC, specific thrust, thrust, thermal efficiency or mission block fuel.
* **Monte Carlo** (`montecarlo.ts`): independent normal perturbations (Mulberry32 PRNG, Box–Muller) of efficiencies, losses and coefficients; distributions of thrust, TSFC, fuel flow, T₃ and thermal efficiency; one-at-a-time +1σ sensitivities.
* **Weight** (`weight.ts`): heuristic component allowances from annulus areas, pressure ratios and temperatures, including a 1.5 structure/accessory multiplier. The total is assembly mass (including the modeled nacelle for turbofans), not certified dry engine mass. It is evaluated only at design; this is not a WATE implementation or an independently validated accuracy bound.
* **Installation** (`core.ts`): additive inlet drag 0.4·q₀·max(A_hl − A₀, 0) with A_hl from the fixed design inlet area and capture-ratio setting, nacelle friction C_f·1.25·q₀·πD L, afterbody drag C_d·q₀·(A_max − A₉).
* **Emissions** (`core.ts`, `certification.ts`): EI_NOx = 32·(P₃/2965 kPa)^0.4·exp((T₃ − 826)/194) g/kg, EI_CO = 0.4 + 40·exp(−(T₃ − 450)/110), EI_HC = 0.05 + 6·exp(−(T₃ − 450)/90), CO₂ from fuel carbon content. ICAO LTO cycle at 100/85/30/7 % of rated sea-level static thrust for 0.7/2.2/4.0/26 min; Dp/Foo against the CAEP/8 NOx limit (piecewise in OPR and rated thrust) and 118 g/kN CO, 19.6 g/kN HC.
* **Noise** (`core.ts`, `certification.ts`): jet mixing OASPL at 1 m = 141 + 10 log₁₀ Σ (ρⱼ/ρ₀)²((Vⱼ − V₀)/a₀)⁸ Aⱼ; fan inlet broadband (uncalibrated heuristic) from fan temperature rise, mass flow and tip Mach, with a tone a few dB above and a buzz-saw note above Mach 1; energy sum for the total. Fan radius is held at its design value off-design. These are nominal source levels without a common polar-angle/directivity model, acoustic treatment or spectra; they are not EPNdB. The 100 m value applies only inverse-distance spreading, without atmosphere or ground effects. The turbojet approximation uses whole-compressor temperature rise, and propeller noise is absent.

**Cooling/life** (`core.ts`): computed coolant demand is limited continuously to a 25 % inlet cooling fraction (plus exit cooling at 0.6/1.6 of that fraction). An unattainable metal-temperature target is flagged; hotter-than-gas coolant cannot satisfy a cooling demand. `thermalLifeProxy_h = 25000·2^((1200−T_metal)/20)` is a temperature-only proxy, not a Larson–Miller stress/material rupture calculation. The former `creepLife_h` output name was corrected.

## 9. Map generators (`custommaps.ts`)

* **Parametric**: elliptical speed lines with stall pressure ratio ∝ Nc^2.1 and a mid-map efficiency peak.
* **Stage stacking**: per stage ψ = ψ*(1 + a(1 − φ/φ*)), parabolic loss bucket, compressibility through density continuity, front-stage variable stators rotating the design flow coefficient with speed.
* **Blade geometry**: per stage mean radius, blade height, rotor inlet/exit metal angles, stator exit angle, solidity. Velocity triangles on the mean line, Carter's deviation δ = 0.23θ/√σ, Lieblein diffusion factor and incidence losses, stall from flow-coefficient and diffusion-factor limits. A consistent-defaults generator sets zero design incidence for a chosen loading and flow coefficient.
* **Turbine from geometry**: nozzle throat area sets the choking flow, flow unchokes below the critical PR, Smith-chart efficiency in loading Δh/U² and flow coefficient.

## 10. Numerical cost

A design-point solve takes about 0.6 ms with frozen chemistry and 16 ms with equilibrium; an off-design Newton solve 5–25 ms frozen. Sweeps, transients, missions, optimisation, Monte Carlo and LTO cycles run in a web worker; the interactive design point runs on the main thread.
